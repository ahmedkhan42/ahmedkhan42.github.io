Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the Ah Comic Studio informational website and the locally operated Windows video-production tool for the Ah comic channel. The channel owner is the contact for privacy and access requests: ahmedkhanpanda@gmail.com.

1. Information the studio handles

  • Production material: story prompts, scripts, character descriptions, generated images, narration, subtitles, video files and upload metadata supplied or created by the operator.
  • YouTube authorization: OAuth client configuration, access tokens and, when issued, refresh tokens used to maintain the connection. The studio does not ask for or store your Google password.
  • Channel and video information: identifiers and information returned by YouTube to verify the connected channel, identify recent videos, upload approved content and check upload processing.
  • Comment drafts: selected recent comment text, comment and video identifiers, locally generated reply drafts, timestamps and review status. The current collector does not post replies.
  • Support correspondence: information you choose to send when contacting the channel owner. Do not include credentials or verification codes.

2. Why this information is used

Information is used to create and review videos, connect the intended channel, carry out an owner-authorized upload or scheduled publication, check processing and prepare comment replies for human review. Google and YouTube data are not sold, used for advertising profiles or submitted to general-purpose AI models for training. The current comment-draft feature uses local rules rather than sending viewer comments to a language model.

3. Services that receive information

The application uses YouTube API Services. Authorized API requests and approved video uploads are sent to Google/YouTube. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When generation is enabled, script prompts are sent to Groq, image prompts and character reference images to Google's Gemini API, and narration text to Microsoft's online speech service through the Edge TTS library. These services are governed by their own applicable terms and privacy notices: Groq, Gemini API and Microsoft. Provider retention and training treatment depend on the service and account settings; this studio does not control those practices. Do not put confidential personal information in production prompts.

4. Local storage and retention

Configuration, OAuth tokens, production files, comments and drafts are stored as files on the operator's computer. The application does not provide a separate encrypted credential vault. Operators must protect the computer and exclude credentials, tokens and viewer data from public repositories and handoff packages.

Production files remain locally until removed. The approved uploader can delete the associated local episode folder after YouTube confirms processing, retaining a small upload receipt. Incomplete uploads leave files in place. Local deletion does not delete a video already uploaded to YouTube.

Comment drafts, receipts and cached API data currently require operator-managed cleanup; there is no automatic retention timer. The operator must delete API data when no longer needed, honor deletion requests and applicable YouTube retention requirements, and not keep stale API data indefinitely. Contact the owner for assistance with copies under the owner's control.

5. Disconnecting and deleting data

  1. Revoke Ah Comic Studio's Google access at Google Account permissions.
  2. Stop scheduled studio tasks and close the application. Remove its local token.json file to disconnect that installation. Revoking access does not automatically erase existing local copies.
  3. Delete the relevant local comment-draft, cached API-data and production files, including copies or backups you control. Keep credentials out of shared archives.
  4. For help removing data held by the channel owner, email a data-deletion request. Identify the affected channel or comment without sending passwords or tokens. The owner must remove relevant API data within seven days of a verified deletion request or consent revocation.

Use YouTube Studio separately to manage or delete published videos and replies. Google or another service may retain information under its own policies after local copies are deleted.

6. This website

These static pages contain no studio login, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded video player or submission form. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, according to the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening external links subjects your visit to that service's policies.

7. Children and changes

The production tool is intended for adult channel owners and authorized adult operators. It is not a service for children under 13. Contact the owner about unintended collection of a child's personal information.

Material changes to data use will be reflected here. New uses of Google data must be explained and any required consent obtained before those uses begin.

Questions, access concerns or deletion help? Contact ahmedkhanpanda@gmail.com.
